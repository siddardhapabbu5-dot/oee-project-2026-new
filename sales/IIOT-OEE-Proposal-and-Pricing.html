1. Phase-1 Proposal (hand this to management)

Keep the first sale small, measurable, and easy to approve.

Phase-1 Goal: One line · 90 days · Visible loss reduction · Expand later

Scope

Included
  • 1 plant · 1 production line
  • Masters: users, shifts, machines, SKUs, downtime reasons
  • Work Order Planning + hourly Production Entries
  • Downtime & Changeover logging
  • OEE dashboards (Plant / Line / Day / Week)
  • Excel reports + role-based access
  • Operator + Supervisor training
  • Weekly review support during Phase-1
Not in Phase-1 (optional later)
  • Multi-line / multi-plant rollout
  • PLC / sensor IIOT auto-capture
  • Custom ERP / SAP integration
  • Mobile app / Andon hardware
  • 24×7 on-site support

Start digital OEE discipline first. IIOT sensors after data quality is proven.

Success criteria (agree in writing)

#CriterionTarget
1Shift data completeness (hourly + downtime)≥ 90%
2OEE dashboard used in daily / weekly meetingYes
3Top downtime reasons ranked by minutesTop 5 visible
4At least one improvement with before/after1–2 projects
5Phase-1 review with Plant HeadEnd of Day 90

90-day timeline

Week 1–2Masters, setup, training
Week 3–4Daily entry discipline
Week 5–8Pareto + first Kaizen
Week 9–12Before/after review → expand

Customer must provide

  • Named champion (Production Manager / Supervisor)
  • Approved OEE policy (startup / lunch / changeover treatment)
  • Standard downtime & quality reason list
  • Weekly 30–45 min review slot with Production + Maintenance

3. How to Decide Your Price (and Your Cost)

If you are unsure of cost, separate your delivery cost from customer selling price.

A. Estimate your delivery cost (internal)

Work itemEffort (Phase-1)Rough internal cost*
Discovery + OEE policy workshop2–3 days₹15–30k
Setup masters + configuration3–5 days₹25–50k
Training (operators + supervisors)2 days₹15–25k
Go-live support (2–4 weeks)part-time₹30–60k
Weekly OEE reviews + report (Phase-1)8–10 calls₹20–40k
Hosting / cloud / domain (Year 1)—₹10–30k
Total internal Phase-1 cost (typical)₹1.0–2.0 Lakh

*Based on blended consulting/dev rate ≈ ₹4–8k/day in India SME services. Use your real salary/overhead.

Pricing rule of thumb: Selling price ≈ 1.5× to 2.5× your delivery cost for Phase-1, and higher margin on Plant Pack once the OEE product is productized. Example: if Phase-1 costs you ₹1.2 Lakh to deliver, quote ₹1.99–2.49 Lakh.

B. Payment terms (recommended)

40%On PO / kickoff
40%On go-live (data entry live)
20%On Phase-1 review (Day 90)

C. What to say when customer asks “Why this price?”

  • “This is not only software — it includes OEE setup, training, and weekly loss reviews.”
  • “Phase-1 is priced to prove OEE value on one line before you invest plant-wide.”
  • “Annual support covers updates, user help, and KPI review cadence.”
  • “IIOT sensor automation is optional Phase-2 after manual data quality is stable.”

D. Discount policy (protect your margin)

Allowed
  • 5–10% for advance full payment
  • Phase-1 credit against Plant Pack (e.g. ₹50–75k)
  • Multi-year AMC prepaid discount
Avoid
  • Free unlimited customisation
  • Open-ended “until management is happy”
  • Cutting training / weekly reviews to win price
