1. Executive Summary & Why OEE

One digital system to monitor production, downtime, quality and OEE — then drive loss reduction.

Objective
Capture hourly production + downtime, calculate OEE automatically, and give management loss visibility by hour, machine, SKU, reason and shift.
Problem today
Data exists in Excel, but losses are hard to analyse quickly. Manual reporting delays visibility and introduces formula / entry errors.
Outcome
Faster decisions, clearer accountability, reduced chronic losses and better line utilisation — with one KPI language across shifts.
OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality
Availability
Operating ÷ Planned Time
Was the line running when it should? Losses: breakdowns, utilities, manpower, material, planned stops.
Performance
Actual vs Ideal Output
Did it run at expected speed? Losses: speed loss, minor stops, adjustments, process variation.
Quality
Good ÷ Total Output
How much good product? Losses: fill, cap, bottle, label, coding, packaging defects.

3. TPM & 4M — From Losses to Projects

OEE identifies losses; TPM converts them into structured improvement projects.

OEE + TPM: OEE = KPI to measure effectiveness. TPM = system to eliminate equipment and process losses (zero breakdowns, zero defects, zero minor stops, operator ownership).

4M root-cause classification

Man
PeopleOperator skill, training, manpower, handling variation
Machine
EquipmentFiller, capper, conveyor, electrical, mechanical, sensors
Material
InputsPreform, bottle, cap, label, water, packaging quality
Method
ProcessSOP, settings, changeover, inspection, startup sequence

Classify every recurring loss → verify with 5-Why / evidence → remove the root cause (not only the symptom).

4. Right First Time (RFT) — Quality that protects OEE

Make product correct the first time — without rework, repair or repeat processing.

RFTFirst-time quality performance
OEE linkImproves the Quality pillar
FocusPrevention, not detection after the fact
RFT % = First-Time-Good Quantity ÷ Total Produced × 100

If 20 cases need rework before acceptance, they are not Right First Time. Define plant rules for rework / reject before using RFT as a KPI.

How OEE + RFT + TPM work together
